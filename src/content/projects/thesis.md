<div style="margin-bottom: 60px;">
  <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 400; color: var(--accent); letter-spacing: -0.02em; margin-bottom: 16px;">Closing the Innovation Loop</h2>
  <p>This research addresses the structural and communicative challenges that arise from rapid organizational scaling within a global enterprise environment. The targeted organization managed a diverse, fast-paced portfolio of customer experiences and events, but found that its rapid expansion had outpaced its structural capacity to process and utilize qualitative stakeholder feedback. This disconnect resulted in a significant 'visibility gap' characterized by asymmetric internal information, isolated executional teams, and a heavy reliance on unvalidated assumptions.</p>
</div>

<!-- ORIGINAL THESIS CONTEXT -->
<div style="margin-bottom: 80px;">
  <p>The core problem mirrored the economic concept of the 'Market for Lemons'—where a lack of information transparency and traceability promotes uncontrolled organizational entropy. Employees were locked into a reactive, short-term operational state, suffering from both temporal and spatial myopia. The primary goal was to uncover hidden systemic behaviors and communication breakdowns without resorting to rigid, top-down auditing that often triggers defensive reactions or data manipulation.</p>
</div>

  <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 400; color: var(--accent); letter-spacing: -0.02em; margin-bottom: 16px;">Prototyping and Execution</h2>
  <p>The design process utilized a pragmatic, non-linear adaptation of the Double Diamond methodology, moving iteratively through research, synthesis, ideation, prototyping, and testing phases.  
  </br></br>
  To operationalize the theoretical framework, I built a functional digital prototype. This tool allows managers to input qualitative data—such as interview transcripts, system maps, and behavioral observations—and utilizes a custom AI-backed backend interpreter to provide quick, insightful systemic analysis. Based on rigorous expert validation and stress-testing, I refined the system to replace numeric scoring with a ranking hierarchy of diagnosed elements. This crucial pivot neutralizes the threat of "performance theater" and ensures the tool acts as an authentic, actionable conversation starter rather than a punitive performance metric.</p>
</div>
